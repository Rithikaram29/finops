# Approval & Sign-off

No discrepancy is considered **resolved**, and no corrective action (reversal,
ledger adjustment, status change) is applied, until a human reviewer approves it.

The agent's role is to detect, classify, explain with citations, and propose a
resolution. The human's role is to approve, reject, or amend. This approval gate
is mandatory for every action that changes financial records, and it applies even
when the agent is highly confident. All approvals and rejections are logged for
audit.
